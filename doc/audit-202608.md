## The finding that reframes the rest

The test suite passed completely, and the instrument was still wrong.

Every DSP test in `tests/test_sample.py` excited the signal chain at 500,
1000, 1500 … 9500 Hz. The configuration under test had a 2.000 Hz FFT bin
spacing. Thus each of those frequencies was exactly on a bin centre. That is
the one degenerate case where the block is periodic, the circular-wrap
discontinuity of the FFT is zero, and the integration error is zero.

The reviewer reproduced this directly. On bin, the integrated displacement
overall was accurate to 0.00%. With the tone 1 Hz off the grid (half a bin),
the same code reported +4474%. Real machines do not run at exact multiples
of the bin width.

Consequence: a green CI is not evidence of measurement correctness in this
repository. The report recommended tests at different frequencies, not more
tests. Three of the top four defects would have been found by one
parametrisation change.

## Fix these first (the report's priority list)

The report ordered these items by measurement risk multiplied by the
probability in normal use. The report confirmed each by code trace or by
numerical reproduction.

1. **Integrated velocity and displacement are corrupted by FFT wrap
   leakage.** See [M-01](#m-01). Proposed fix: Hann taper and power-gain
   correction before the integrating FFT; about 4 lines; verified to reduce
   +4474% to +0.00%.
2. **The spectral anomaly hook fires on every healthy dataset.**
   `anomaly.py:302` triggers on `np.any(|spec − baseline| / baseline > 0.5)`
   across every bin in the band (thousands of bins). Welch runs a single
   segment in 56 of 72 preset combinations. Thus each noise-floor bin is
   χ²(2)-distributed, with a standard deviation equal to its mean.
   P(some bin exceeds 1.5×) ≈ 1.0 on each frame, so `consecutive_n` gives no
   protection. Observed to fire at frame 18, the earliest possible index, on
   healthy synthetic data. Proposed fix: compare band RMS, not bins; require
   the same band on consecutive frames; threshold in σ, not a fixed %;
   default band `highpass_fc` … `maxfreq`.
   This item has no ID of its own. [S-02](#s-02) calls it "M-02 in the
   priority list". Status: **Open (R39).** The GUI does not offer the hook
   since `940b08a`; headless still accepts it.
3. **The reported sample rate is not the sample rate used.** See
   [M-02](#m-02).
4. **Closing the app during a recording corrupts the session and strands
   the device.** See [S-01](#s-01).

## Measurement validity

The report: "Nine independent defects that put a wrong number in front of
the user." This is the largest risk category for an instrument, and the
category that the test suite covered least.

### M-01

**FFT wrap leakage corrupts every integrated overall and waveform.**

- Severity: Critical. Other ID: F-1.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `collector.py:472-473`, `497-521`, `557-579`.

The integrating FFT at `collector.py:472` operated on a raw block with no
window. A multiplication by (jω)ⁿ with n < 0 amplifies the wrap
discontinuity by 1/ωᵏ. Zeroing bins 0 and 1 removed the worst of it, but the
leakage in bins 2, 3, 4 … stayed and was amplified. The code comment said
that the round trip was lossless; that was true only for n = 0.

This corrupted the result card, the trend plot, the saved `overall_json`,
the input of the anomaly detector, and the displayed waveform. The spectrum
was correct.

Velocity from an accelerometer (the most usual configuration in this
industry) read +1.8% mean bias, up to +12%, with 1.2% scatter from frame to
frame on a steady machine. Displacement read +123% mean, with single frames
up to 20×. The error depends on frequency and phase. Thus it shows as noise
on the trend, not as a constant offset that a calibration can remove.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `75e5f9a` (merge `b4a67dc`), CHANGELOG `fix/measurement-validity
  (2026-08-28)`. Scalar overalls use a Hann taper with power-gain
  correction. The displayed waveform uses Tukey overlap-save. Measured with
  synthetic signals after the fix: displacement at 501.0 Hz +4473.76% →
  +0.00%; velocity at 61.0 Hz +12.25% → +0.05%.

### M-02

**Frequency axis is 1.73% low across all presets ≥ 2 kHz (8.99% at 50 kHz).**

- Severity: High. Other ID: F-2.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `picoscope.py:593-620`.

`picoscope.py:611` computed the rate that the driver delivered. Then
`picoscope.py:620` discarded it: `self._actual_samplerate =
self.config.samplerate`. The driver quantised the sample interval to whole
microseconds. Thus at F_max = 2 kHz the hardware ran at 8333.33 Hz and the
app labelled it 8192 Hz.

A true 100 Hz tone displayed at 98.3 Hz; 60 Hz line frequency read 59.0 Hz.
Harmonic family identification, sideband spacing, and BPFO/BPFI comparison
against a nameplate were all off by 1.73%, and by 8.99% at the 50 kHz
preset. The integration, which uses the labelled f, also had a bias.
Proposed fix: `self._actual_samplerate = actual_raw_fs /
self._effective_osr`.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `270d36e` (merge `b4a67dc`), CHANGELOG `fix/measurement-validity`.
  Hardware check (PicoScope 4424A, AWG loopback): a 1000.00 Hz tone read
  983.062 Hz before and 1000.012 Hz after. Later, `7e63dcc` (CHANGELOG
  `experimental/profiling`) snaps the streaming interval to the 12.5 ns
  clock grid of the device. The achieved raw rate is then 25591.81 Hz
  (−320 ppm), measured on a 4824A.

### M-03

**No anti-alias filtering at all above F_max = 20 kHz.**

- Severity: High. Other ID: F-3.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `picoscope.py:319-321`, `56-57`; `util.py:49`.

`int(min(OSR_TARGET, STREAMING_CEILING_HZ / samplerate))` truncated to 1 at
fs = 65536 (100000/65536 = 1.526). That made `antialias_decimate` do
nothing. At the 50 kHz preset the ratio was 0.763 → `max(1, 0)` = 1. Also,
the requested raw rate was 31% above the measured throughput ceiling of the
module. The module docstring says that this condition makes the driver drop
most samples while it still reports `status='OKAY'`.

A general-purpose IEPE accelerometer has a mounted resonance at 25–80 kHz,
with 20–30 dB of gain. At F_max = 20 kHz a 50 kHz resonance folds to
15536 Hz. That is inside the displayed band, and it looks like real signal.
The 50 kHz preset streamed degraded 100% of the time.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `10940d6` (merge `b4a67dc`), CHANGELOG `fix/measurement-validity`.
  `_choose_osr()` requires `osr >= 2`. The 20 kHz and 50 kHz presets are
  removed; `MAXFREQ_PRESETS` stops at 10 kHz. The CHANGELOG gives the raw
  rate that the 50 kHz preset requested: 131072 Hz.

### M-04

**High-pass filter state is reset to zero on every block.**

- Severity: High. Other ID: F-4.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `collector.py:462-467`.

`sosfilt` was called with no `zi`. Thus a start-up transient came back at
the head of each frame of a continuous stream.

With zero DC offset, the transient alone put the displayed acceleration
waveform 12.3% above the true peak, velocity 69%, and displacement 528%.
It scales with the input offset. At 1000 mV of residual DC (possible on DC
coupling or after a range change), the acceleration overall read +78% high
and velocity +467% high. No function discarded frames while an offset
settled.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `e91f230` (state carried across blocks) and `9937cd4` (seed from
  the block mean, found on hardware), merge `b4a67dc`, CHANGELOG
  `fix/measurement-validity`. The settling part is [S-13](#s-13).

### M-05

**Overload and degraded frames are trended, alarmed on, and lose their flags
on save.**

- Severity: High. Other ID: F-5.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `collector.py:611-613`, `890`, `1190`, `1224`;
  `monitor/writer.py:21-31`.

Four gaps in one chain:

1. `update_trend` ran regardless of `overflow` or `degraded`.
2. The anomaly hooks did not read either flag.
3. `_read_frame_group` hard-coded `overflow=False`, so the stored flag was
   never read back.
4. The monitor writer stored no validity flags.

Also, the overflow mask was not latched across the accumulation window. An
overflow in a callback that did not complete a block was lost.

This is the usual spurious-alarm mechanism. A clipped waveform reads high
with harmonic distortion, the trend records a step, and the detector fires
a burst. On reload the analyst sees a clean record.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `d0bc13e` (merge `b4a67dc`), CHANGELOG `fix/measurement-validity`.
  The flags are latched with `|=`. Flagged frames are shown but not
  trended. The flags are read back. The monitor writer uses the same
  `_write_channel_group` as `save_data`. `valid_results()` filters the
  hooks.

### M-06

**The overall is not band-limited: it spans up to 2.05× the user's F_max.**

- Severity: High.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `collector.py:497-521`; `sample.py:107-124`.

The overall RMS was taken over the whole filtered block. Thus its band was
`highpass_fc` … fs/2, and fs/2 was 2.048 × F_max at the 500, 1000 and
2000 Hz presets. There was no band definition, no upper edge tied to F_max,
and no ISO 20816 band.

Content that the user excluded still reached the trend. 2 g RMS at 1500 Hz,
outside a 1000 Hz F_max, changed the reported overall velocity from 3.00 to
3.74 mm/s (+25%). That is sufficient to move a machine from ISO Zone B to
Zone C. Overalls taken at different F_max were also not comparable, which
makes long-term trends invalid.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `65622d3` and `6449477` (merge `76e8f9f`), CHANGELOG
  `round 2: vibration-analysis integrity (2026-08-29)`. The overall is
  measured over the declared band (`band_fmin` … `band_fmax`, default
  `highpass_fc` … `maxfreq`). Hardware check (4424A, AWG loopback, band
  10–1000 Hz at F_max 2000 Hz): 1100 Hz reads −58.2 dB.

### M-07

**Pre-trigger burst overalls are unscaled, typically 100× high.**

- Severity: High.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `monitor/controller.py:258`.

The code read `s_cfg.get('sensitivity_mv_per_eu', 1.0)`, but
`ScopeSensor.to_dict()` writes the key `sensitivity`. The misspelled key
existed nowhere else. Thus the 1.0 default always applied and the mV→EU
division did not occur. `engineering_units` on the next line was correct,
so the unit label changed but the magnitude did not.

The pre-trigger trend points of every burst were 100× the post-trigger
points on the same plot. The error stayed after the HDF5 round trip.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `d8a4e3f` (merge `ebaa6f9`), CHANGELOG
  `chore/config-consistency-ci (2026-08-28)`. The controller rebuilds each
  sensor through `ScopeSensor.from_dict()`.

### M-08

**The acquisition dialog computes the sample rate with 2×, not 2.56×.**

- Severity: Medium. Other ID: F-6.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `gui.py:771` against `sample.py:119`.

The dialog had its own copy of the formula, and the copy was wrong. At the

2000 Hz presets.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `e4628fd` (merge `b4a67dc`), CHANGELOG `fix/measurement-validity`.

  `AcquisitionSettings`.

### M-09

**The PSD cache key omits binsize, so the spectrum goes stale.**

- Severity: Medium. Other ID: F-7.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `collector.py:476-479`.

`nfft` depends on `binsize` and `samplerate`. The cache key had neither.
Verified: a change from 2 Hz to 0.5 Hz bins returned the same 2049-point,
2 Hz spectrum. Streaming hid it, because each new sample starts with
`psd_mv = None`.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `1ccc4f2` (merge `b4a67dc`), CHANGELOG `fix/measurement-validity`.

### M-10

**Stated line count and bin width do not match the computed spectrum.**

- Severity: Medium. Other ID: F-8.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `sample.py:154-157`; `gui.py:735-736`.

`n_fft_bins` returned `blocksize//2 + 1`, but Welch used `nfft =
int(samplerate/binsize)`, and `blocksize ≥ nfft`. In the 72-combination
preset grid, 40 combinations had a line-count mismatch or a bin-width error
above 1%. Worst case: F_max = 2000 Hz, df = 5 Hz claimed 1025 lines against
an actual 820. `tests/test_acquisition_settings.py:82-84` asserted the
incorrect value.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `1ccc4f2` (merge `b4a67dc`), CHANGELOG `fix/measurement-validity`.
  `nperseg = blocksize`. After the fix, 0 of 72 combinations mismatch.

### M-11

**Spectrum is displayed out to fs/2, where alias rejection is about 12 dB.**

- Severity: Medium. Other ID: F-9.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `gui.py:347-352`, `903-911`.

The X axis went to 1.28 × F_max. The measured attenuation at the frequency
that folds into the top of the band was −21.8 dB, and about 0 dB at fs/2.
The F_max = fs/2.56 convention exists so that the guard band is never
shown. `find_peaks` and the peaks table included this region. The info
label called fs/2 the "AA" frequency, which reads as a specification that
the instrument does not meet.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `0e61153` (merge `b4a67dc`), CHANGELOG `fix/measurement-validity`.

### M-12

**Anti-alias stopband is about 55 dB against an expectation of about 80 dB.**

- Severity: Medium.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `picoscope.py:250-259`.

`decimate(ftype='fir', zero_phase=True)` calls `resample_poly` with a
Hamming kernel, whose sidelobes are at about −53 dB. Measured −55.5 dB
worst case into the passband. ISO 2954 and analyser practice call for
80 dB or more. This limits the usable dynamic range to 55 dB, whatever the
14/16-bit ADC resolution. A Kaiser(8.6) kernel reaches about −90 dB with a
longer FIR.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `65622d3` (merge `76e8f9f`), CHANGELOG `round 2`. Kaiser design,
  100 dB, 0.20 transition, 259 taps at q = 4: −111.7 dB measured, against
  −60.0 dB for the Hamming kernel in the same measurement.

### M-13

**The DSP tests only probe bin-centred frequencies with the high-pass off.**

- Severity: Medium.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `tests/test_sample.py:6`, `19-31`.

This is the root cause that let M-01 reach production. Tone frequencies
were exact multiples of the bin width. `_make_dc` set
`highpass_enabled=False`. The displacement tests asserted only on the
spectrum peak, never on `result.overall`. A 20× displacement error passed
CI.

The report called this the change with the highest leverage in the
repository: put the tone at `freq + 0.37*df`, enable the high-pass in one
variant, and assert on `result.overall`. That one change catches M-01 and
M-04.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `93d9f36` (failing baseline: 110 tests, 92 failing) and the
  M-01 to M-11 fixes, merge `b4a67dc`, CHANGELOG `fix/measurement-validity`.
  `tests/test_measurement_validity.py` is off-bin, uses phase 0.7 rad, and
  enables the high-pass. `tests/test_sample.py` stays on-bin on purpose.
  New amplitude assertions do not go there.

### M-14

**The simulated bearing signal is not a valid oracle.**

- Severity: Medium.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `simulation.py:51-74`.

The "bearing defect" was ten pure cosines plus white noise: kurtosis ≈ 3,
that is, Gaussian. A real defect is an impulse train that excites a
structural resonance, amplitude-modulated by the load zone, with 1–2% slip
jitter. The synthetic signal had no impulses, no resonance carrier, no
modulation sidebands and no slip. Thus it could not validate envelope
analysis, crest factor, kurtosis or leakage behaviour.
`GenerateBearingVibration_SpectralMethod` also had an operator-precedence
bug at `:43`, which gave a wrong loop bound.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `7cbb132` (merge `d59470f`), CHANGELOG `round 2`.
  `GenerateBearingVibration()` is the default simulated source. The loop
  bound is `bearing_harmonic_count()`.

## Stability and data loss

The report: these findings are concentrated in `monitor/`, the newest code
(about 1800 lines), which CLAUDE.md did not document at that time. Some of
them compound with one another.

### S-01

**Monitor session is truncated on app close; render-loop exceptions strand
the device.**

- Severity: Critical.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `gui.py:3691-3701`; `__main__.py:59-61`;
  `monitor/writer.py:56`.

Two defects compound. `GUI.cleanup()` at `gui.py:3701` did not call
`self._monitor.stop()`, and the writer is a daemon thread. Thus the
interpreter killed the writer, possibly inside `h5py.File(…, 'a')`, with
queued captures not written. Also, the loop body of `run()` had no
`try/except` and `__main__.main()` had no `try/finally`. Thus an exception
in the render path skipped `cleanup()`, and `ps4000aCloseUnit` did not run.

Result: an overnight monitoring session is truncated or unreadable, and the
next launch gets `PICO_NOT_FOUND` until the USB cable is disconnected and
connected again. Proposed fix: stop the monitor in `cleanup()`; put the
render-loop body in `try/except`; put `run()` in `try/finally:
app.cleanup()`.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `ae576e2` (merge `e8e24a3`), CHANGELOG `fix/stability-cluster`.
  `cleanup()` stops the monitor first, then closes the device, then
  destroys the dearpygui context. Each step has its own guard, and
  `cleanup()` can run more than once.

### S-02

**Three defects compound into an out-of-memory kill on unattended runs.**

- Severity: Critical.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `monitor/controller.py:310-321`;
  `monitor/writer.py:54`, `72-84`.

Each part alone is High. Together they are the most probable way for an
overnight session to stop.

- (a) The spectral hook ("M-02 in the priority list"; see
  [Fix these first](#fix-these-first-the-reports-priority-list), item 2)
  fires a burst on almost every frame.
- (b) Burst capture kept every frame and every `ChannelResult` (about 3× the
  raw block each) with no cap. `max_burst_s` was enforced only inside
  `IntervalGate.enter_burst`, and the anomaly path did not call it. At
  F_max = 50 kHz that is about 3840 frames × 4 channels × about 130 KB,
  approximately 2 GB before the single flush.
- (c) The writer queue had no `maxsize`. Thus its `except queue.Full` branch
  could not run, and `enqueue` always returned True. That return increments
  the capture counter, so the UI reported captures that were never written.

On the documented Raspberry Pi headless target this is an OOM kill during
the session. If the disk guard trips first, the writer stops but the
controller continues to put items into a queue that nothing reads. The GUI
did not act on the error flag; headless did.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `59723d4` (merge `e8e24a3`), CHANGELOG `fix/stability-cluster`:
  bounded queue that drops and counts (`dropped_captures`), shared
  `capped_burst_end()`, and `burst_frame_cap()` with frames and results
  trimmed together. Then `a216ad9`, CHANGELOG `feature/tachometer — one
  session factory` (2026-09-21): both front ends read `max_burst_s` from the
  config. Before that, both passed a literal 600.0. Measured after the fix
  (4 channels, `RAW_SAMPLERATE_HZ` = 25600): retention 2.76× the raw block,
  growth 2.26 MB/s (about 8.1 GB/h) without a cap. Part (a) stays open as
  R39 for headless.

### S-13

Start and watchdog recovery both reset the accumulator and send blocks

## Security

The report found no critical or high security findings. Every YAML read
uses `safe_load`. The tree has no `pickle`, `eval`, `exec`, `shell=True` or
committed secret. The atomic-write implementation is correct, with no
TOCTOU or symlink exposure. The one finding with real effect is a data-loss
path, not an intrusion.

### X-01

**The sensor library can be silently and permanently destroyed, by two
independent routes.**

- Severity: Medium on security grounds; High in effect.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `config.py:159`; `scope_sensor_registry.py:39-40`;
  `README.md:904`.

There is no code execution, but the user loses every calibrated sensor
definition. Two reviewers found two triggers with one root cause:
`_load_user` swallowed any parse failure with `except Exception: return
[]`, and `_save_user` then wrote that empty list over the file.

- Route 1, documentation. `README.md:904` told users to add sensors with
  the key `sensitivity_mv_per_eu`. The real key is `sensitivity`. Thus
  `from_dict` raised `KeyError`, the library read as empty, every channel
  went back to raw mV scaling, and the next add or delete erased the file.
  Nothing was logged. This is the same wrong key as M-07.
- Route 2, shared measurement files. `_atomic_yaml_write` used `yaml.dump`
  with the unsafe default Dumper, while every reader used `safe_load`. To
  open a colleague's `.h5` registered its sensors in the global library with
  no prompt and no type coercion. A non-scalar attribute was written as a
  `!!python/object/apply:` tag that `safe_load` then refused (reproduced end
  to end). No remote code execution today. But a future change to
  `yaml.load` would make this arbitrary code execution from a shared file.

Proposed fix: `yaml.safe_dump`; coerce every field in
`ScopeSensor.from_dict`; log and skip a bad entry, not the list; correct
`README.md:904`; ask before a change to the global library from an opened
file.

- Status: **Fixed.**
- Fixed by: `9df51af` and `d8a4e3f` (merge `ebaa6f9`), CHANGELOG
  `chore/config-consistency-ci`. `safe_dump`; `from_dict` coerces and
  rejects containers; a bad entry is logged and skipped; a bad file raises,
  so a read failure cannot become an overwrite; README and CLAUDE.md use
  `sensitivity`.
- Not done: the proposed prompt. `GUI._wire_session_sensors` still adds
  unknown sensors from an opened file to the library, and logs each one.
  The data-loss routes are closed, so this file records the finding as
  fixed.

### X-05

**Device handle can be closed while the poll thread is inside the driver.**

- Severity: Medium.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `picoscope.py:402-429`, `626-696`.

`stop()` joined with a 3 s timeout, logged the failure, and then called
`ps4000aStop` and `ps4000aCloseUnit`, while the poll thread was possibly
still in `ps4000aGetStreamingLatestValues` on the same handle. A close and
a read at the same time on one handle is undefined behaviour in the driver.
Also, `noOfSamples` had no bounds check. Thus a bad driver value makes the
assignment raise inside a ctypes callback, where the exception cannot
propagate, and the C caller gets an indeterminate return.

The report also said what is correct: `picosdk` sets `argtypes` on every
symbol, so out-of-range values raise and do not truncate. The registered
buffer length, the allocation and `overviewBufferSize` are all consistent at
1000 samples. The ring-buffer wrap handling is correct.

- Status: **Open. Tracked as R56 in `doc/PROGRESS.md`.**
- Checked on 2026-09-29: `PicoScopeStream.stop()` still joins with
  `timeout=3.0`, logs "poll thread did not exit cleanly", and calls
  `ps4000aStop` in all cases; `close()` then calls `ps4000aCloseUnit`.
  `_streaming_callback` has no upper bound check on `noOfSamples`.
  `99dbc1b` (CHANGELOG `fix/stability-cluster`) adds `faulthandler`, which
  records a driver crash if one occurs. It does not prevent one.

### X-06

**DLL-path hardening is silently disabled in development.**

### H-08

**Assorted correctness nits.**

- Severity: Low.
- Location at `7bf4712`: `sample.py` (`copy`); `collector.py`;
  `monitor/controller.py`; `.python-version`.

1. `AcquisitionSettings.copy()` copied only maxfreq and binsize, and
   dropped every per-channel dict. No callers in `src/`; one in tests.
2. `datetime.utcnow()` is deprecated on the Python version of the project.

   flush in the same second collide, and the second is dropped. In

- Item 4 open, checked on 2026-09-29: `MonitorController.trigger_burst` and

## What was sound

The report stated this plainly, because the finding count is not the full
picture, and none of it argues for a rewrite.

- **Architecture.** `AcquisitionSettings` derives `samplerate` and
  `blocksize` as read-only properties, so the coupled invariants cannot be
  bypassed. `ChannelResult` is a frozen dataclass. The hardware thread
  never touches dearpygui. The event plus ring cache is the correct
  primitive for "GUI slower than the data rate".
  `get_trend_for_display()` keeps all unit and sensitivity conversion out
  of the view layer.
- **Measurement chain.** Correct wherever the findings do not say
  otherwise. ADC counts → mV, the coherent-gain handling of Welch, the
  frequency-domain integration of the spectrum, and mV → EU → target unit
  were verified numerically to within 0.001%. A 1.000 g tone reads
  1.000001 g. There is no factor-of-2, no √2 and no mV/V ×1000 error in the
  amplitude path. The errors were leakage and labelling, not scaling.
- **Security.** `safe_load` everywhere; no pickle, eval or shell execution;
  a correct atomic write; correct ctypes `argtypes`; consistent driver
  buffer lengths; disk-space guards before writes; no committed secrets.
  The hardware constants in `picoscope.py` carry the measurements that
  justify them.

The report's confidence note: the reviewers' numbers matched the
cross-check to the digit, so confidence in the reported findings is high.
Confidence that the set is complete is lower, because the untested areas
(thread shutdown, watchdog recovery, the monitor state machine) are where
the confirmed defects cluster.
